Salon operations · v1 structure

Braidflow

One queue, three views. The front desk checks a walk-in in under ten seconds; the braider only ever sees her own chair; the owner sees the day. Everything else is derived from a single customer status.

The one state machine
WAITING
→
IN PROGRESS
→
COMPLETED
Trigger 1Supervisor checks in a walk-in → queue number issued. Trigger 2Auto-allocation to the longest-idle free braider (supervisor can override before or after). Trigger 3Braider taps Finish client → she flips to Free and the next waiting customer is pulled to her chair.

Braider availability is never entered by hand — it is a read of "does this braider have an in-progress customer, and is she clocked in". That's what keeps the two journeys in sync.

No separate "availability" table. Braider status = derived; queue position = order by checked_in_at where status is waiting.

Assumptions I made — correct me
· Phone-first, 6 braiders, one location, one shared device at the desk + braiders on their own phones.
· Only the braid style is captured at check-in; price/deposit is out of scope for v1.
· Auto-assign is on by default and picks the longest-idle clocked-in braider; the supervisor can reassign at any point.
· A braider can hold exactly one in-progress customer. Long styles (4–6h) are the norm, so wait estimates come from service averages, not a fixed slot length.
· Walk-ins only. No advance booking, no payments, no SMS yet.
Next steps: add SMS "you're next" notifications · deposit capture at check-in · skill-matched allocation (style → eligible braiders) · weekly owner report · multi-branch.
